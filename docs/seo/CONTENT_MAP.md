## Highest-value content additions (need client input, not copywriting)

1. **Product facts:** active ingredient and dosage form for all 27 products. This is what healthcare professionals and distributors search for, and what AI systems cite. It unlocks medical structured data too (SCHEMA_PLAN).
2. **Business cooperation specifics:**
   - capacity per dosage form;
   - certificates (GMP and issuer);
   - the typical engagement process (enquiry → technical review → registration support → supply).

   This is the page most likely to earn B2B leads, and today it has no numbers.
3. **Downloadable company profile (PDF)** for distributors, if Itqan has one.
