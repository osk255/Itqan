## What this means for Itqan

1. **The winning pattern is a dedicated, specific B2B page.** The strongest pages (Hikma, MS Pharma, Amman Pharma) state concrete capability: dosage forms, plants, capacity, criteria and process. Itqan's `/business-cooperation/` has the structure but no numbers. Capacity and certificates are the biggest content gap (CONTENT_MAP).
2. **Directories carry entity authority.** Itqan is already in JAPM, D&B and Pharmchoices, but some details are inconsistent. Fixing name, address and phone (NAP) consistency is cheap and helps both Google and AI systems corroborate the company.
3. **Don't copy the Indian CMOs' country landing pages.** Country doorway pages are prohibited (constitution §46). One strong cooperation page with the approvals list is the right answer.
4. **The name is ambiguous.** "Itqan" is shared with unrelated firms in Saudi Arabia, Qatar, the UAE and Egypt. Consistent use of "Itqan Pharmaceutical Industries" plus Organization schema with `sameAs` helps disambiguate. See AI_VISIBILITY_PLAN.
