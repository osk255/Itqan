# Preview feedback

Use this during the Phase 1b feedback round. Open-ended "what do you think?" gets vague answers. Specific prompts get feedback you can act on.

## Send reviewers this

> Here's a preview of the new Itqan Pharma website: **<netlify URL>**
> It's a design prototype, so it's slower than the final site will be. Please don't submit the contact form (it goes to Itqan's real inbox).
> Try it on your phone and, if you can, on a laptop. Toggle Light/Dark at the top right.
>
> 1. What does Itqan do? Answer in one sentence, after 10 seconds on the home page.
> 2. If you were a distributor or a company wanting a manufacturing partner, where would you click first? Was it easy?
> 3. Find **Etoria** (or any product). How long did it take? Was anything missing that you expected to see?
> 4. Does it feel trustworthy for a pharmaceutical company? What adds trust, and what takes it away?
> 5. Did any animation feel slow, distracting or broken? Especially the 3D logo when scrolling the home page.
> 6. Anything hard to read, too small to tap, or cut off on your phone?
> 7. One thing you'd change.

## Log

Record each point separately. Bucket is one of: **design** (back to Claude Design), **production** (handled in Phase 2 anyway, e.g. speed), **content** (goes to [CLIENT_QUESTIONS.md](CLIENT_QUESTIONS.md)), **won't do** (give a reason).
